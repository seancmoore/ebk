# EBK Full Refresh — Research Brief (CONFIRMED 2026-07-28)

## Product
Free multi-sport trivia — grids, streaks, stat lines, career paths, live 1v1, leaderboards
across NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, CFB, EPL. Trajectory: great web app now → ad-monetized mobile app.

## Job to be done
Two moments: (1) 3-minute boredom filler anywhere; (2) morning daily ritual with a shareable
result. Ads model ⇒ retention IS revenue. Design for coming back, not session length.

## Audience
Sports fans who live in group chats. Casual play, but knowing ball is identity.

## Feel / Archetype
Warm, clever, communal — puzzle club, not sports bar. Reference feel: NYT Games
(respect the player, clean surfaces, shareable results).
Archetype: **Everyman (belonging), Jester in copy only.** Retires earlier Hero read.
"Prove it" survives as banter, not aggression.

## Taste pins (dealer)
NONE on hue/family — everything on the table. Navy/green NOT protected.
The only pin is *change itself*: any candidate that reads like the current site fails.

## Surfaces
Stated "both equally"; executed as mobile-first components, desktop spends the extra space.
Ad slots reserved in layouts from day one.

## Constraints
Static Firebase hosting, vanilla JS/CSS, ~10 real templates (49 pages).
Leaky token system: 44 tokens in base.css but 51 stray hexes, 86 inline styles,
83 JS-styled markup sites. Font is system-stack (Segoe UI) — replace everywhere.
Ethics: deceptive-patterns doctrine applies to ads + streak mechanics.
No guilt-tripping, no fake urgency.

## Off-site surfaces in scope
icon.svg, og.png, ball SVGs, favicon, IG avatar + grid templates.

## REVISION 1 (2026-07-28, post-mock)
Almanac mock (Warm Editorial × Marginalia) REJECTED on sight. Real taste pin surfaced:
**retro sports + arcade feel** (think scoreboard/coin-op/NBA-Jam energy, vintage athletic
graphics). "Warm clever communal" was menu-picked, not felt — superseded.
Archetype re-read: Jester/Explorer energy, communal retained, banter copy retained.
Dealer pin: family=retro-futurist. Daily-ritual JTBD, mobile-first, ads model unchanged.
